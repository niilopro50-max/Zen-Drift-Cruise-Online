Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This page explains what information Zen Drift Cruise uses when you play, create an account or sync your game progress, and how to contact us about your information.

Information used by the game

You can play without an account. If you create or use an account, the game processes your email address and account identifier to sign you in and associate your save with you. When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase handle the authentication exchange; the game uses the account email and identifier for the signed-in session. The game does not receive or store your account password.

If you enable cloud saves, the game stores the driver name, selected car, paint colors per car, drift-car skin, best Time Attack result and its optional ghost-driving sample on your account. These details are also stored in your browser or device so your local game can remember your settings and progress.

Leaderboard sharing is on by default for signed-in accounts. Your driver name, best Time Attack time and Endless playtime are visible to anyone viewing the game leaderboard. You can switch sharing off in Settings; while signed in, this removes your public entries.

While a game is running, it sends basic gameplay state to the game server, such as speed, drift angle, elapsed time, distance, road surface and whether the car is on mud. This helps the online game operate. The server keeps current gameplay state in memory and does not use it as part of your account save.

Browser storage

The game uses browser storage for local settings and progress, including your driver name, steering choice, control bindings, graphics and accessibility preferences, garage setup, best time and ghost record. Supabase also stores the sign-in session in browser storage so you can stay signed in. Clearing this website's stored data in your browser removes locally saved settings and signs you out; cloud saves remain in your account until deleted.

Optional website statistics are off until you choose “Allow stats.” If you allow them, the site stores a random identifier for the current browser session and records which main pages were opened, active time, broad device type, the referring website's host name and clicks on download links. It does not collect your name, email address, full referrer URL or IP address. Detailed session records are deleted after up to 12 months; anonymous aggregate totals without session identifiers are retained indefinitely to show overall statistics. You can reject or change this choice at any time using the preference control below.

Service providers

We use the following providers to operate the game:

Supabase provides account authentication, the database for cloud saves and, if you allow statistics, anonymous website usage counts. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
Render hosts the website and game server. Render may process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the service. See the Render Privacy Policy.
Google receives sign-in requests if you choose Continue with Google and provides the authentication response through Supabase. See Google's Privacy Policy.
jsDelivr delivers the Supabase browser library used for account features. When that library loads, your browser connects to jsDelivr. See the jsDelivr privacy information.

Cloud saves are protected by database access rules that restrict each signed-in account to its own save. Data handled by providers is also subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local settings stay in your browser until you clear the website's stored data. Cloud account and save data remain while the account is active. Detailed anonymous website session records are retained for up to 12 months; aggregate statistics without session identifiers are retained indefinitely. To request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated cloud save, contact us using the address below. We may need enough information to verify the request. Provider backups or security records may remain for a limited time under the provider's retention practices or where required by law.

Children

Account creation is optional. Please contact us if you believe a child has created an account and you want us to review or remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the game or its data practices change. The effective date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.

Contact

For game support, privacy questions or account-data requests, email zendriftcruise@gmail.com.
